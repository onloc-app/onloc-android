New backend! The whole API has been rebuilt from scratch using NestJS because the old one was filled with bugs and became so heavy it was hard to maintain.

This update adapts the app to this new backends and brings a couple of changes:
- When using real-time location updates, unless someone is currently watching your device, updates will be stored in batches and sent every 15 minutes.
- UnifiedPush now registers user wide instead of per device.
